## Learnings
- A rig step that types after a fixed sleep measures nothing on a 68020: nvim was still busy,
  the keys queued, and ran later. Poll a file nvim writes.
- Host time x ~1500-1850 is a fair first estimate of 68020 time for nvim's own work.
- PUC Lua 5.1 on a 32-bit target truncates every 64-bit lua_pushinteger.
- vsh runs programs without SetProgramDir (request V3 in the ledger); do not trust
  GetProgramDir() alone.
